# Researcher in GeoAI and Remote Sensing

I am a Research Scholar and Training Lead at SAR.Sense Geointelligence Lab, working at the intersection of **GeoAI, remote sensing, machine learning, climate science, and environmental research**.

My research focuses on applying geospatial data, Earth observation, machine learning, and explainable AI to understand **climate extremes, natural hazards, environmental change, and land-surface processes**. My work spans Bangladesh, South Asia, the Arabian Peninsula, and other regions.

I am particularly interested in developing data-driven and interpretable approaches for understanding the **spatial drivers of environmental change and climate-related hazards**.
